Different types of testing offer different perspectives:

- **Black box**: No prior knowledge (simulates an outside attacker).
- **White box**: Full knowledge (architecture, source), for thorough coverage.
- **Grey box**: Partial knowledge (e.g., a normal user account).

Related engagements:
- **Vulnerability assessment**: Breadth (finding weaknesses).
- **Penetration test**: Depth (exploiting weaknesses to prove impact).
- **Red team**: Adversary simulation testing the **entire defense** (people, process, and tech).
- **Bug bounty**: Crowdsourced testing under a published authorization policy.
