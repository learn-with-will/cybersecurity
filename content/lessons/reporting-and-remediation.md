The exploit is **not** the deliverable — the **report** is. 

A good report provides:
- A clear description of the finding.
- Reproduction steps and evidence.
- A risk rating (impact + likelihood).
- Concrete remediation guidance.

Findings are prioritized so the owner fixes the most dangerous issues first. The final step is a **retest** to confirm the fixes actually work. Value is created when vulnerabilities get **fixed**, not when they're found.
