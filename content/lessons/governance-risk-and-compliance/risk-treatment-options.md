# Risk Treatment Options

Once risks are assessed, treat them by choosing one of four responses:

- **Mitigate (reduce)**: Add controls to lower likelihood or impact (the most common choice).
- **Transfer (share)**: Shift risk to another party, like through insurance or outsourcing.
- **Avoid**: Stop the risky activity entirely.
- **Accept**: Consciously accept a risk within your **risk appetite**, and document it in the **risk register**.

Whatever remains after controls is **residual risk**, which leadership must knowingly accept.

## Remember
- There are four standard ways to treat risk: mitigate, transfer, avoid, and accept.
- Residual risk must be consciously accepted by leadership.
- A risk register tracks all identified risks and their chosen treatments.
