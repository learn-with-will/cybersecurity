# Governance Basics

**Governance** is the system of direction and accountability for security: leadership sets objectives, assigns ownership (like a **CISO**), allocates resources, and measures results.

It is expressed as a document hierarchy:

- **Policy**: WHAT and WHY ("All data must be encrypted.")
- **Standard**: specific rules ("Use TLS 1.2+ and AES-256.")
- **Procedure**: step-by-step ("How to install a TLS certificate.")
- **Guideline**: recommended ("Prefer automated renewal.")

Good governance makes security a **business function with an owner and a budget**, not an afterthought.

## Remember
- Governance provides direction, priorities, and accountability for security.
- The document hierarchy flows from high-level policies to specific procedures.
- Effective governance integrates security into the business function.
