# Frameworks and Regulations

Organizations use **frameworks** so they don't have to invent a security program from scratch, and follow **regulations** that apply to their industry.

- **NIST CSF 2.0**: A flexible framework focused on six functions: Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, and Recover.
- **ISO/IEC 27001**: International standard for an Information Security Management System (ISMS).
- **SOC 2**: An audit report on service provider controls based on Trust Services Criteria.
- **PCI DSS**: Mandatory security requirements for handling payment card data.
- **HIPAA**: Protects health information in the U.S.

## Remember
- Frameworks are maps that organize your security work, not guarantees of security.
- Regulations are mandatory requirements that differ by industry and location.
- NIST CSF and ISO 27001 are common backbones for building a program.
