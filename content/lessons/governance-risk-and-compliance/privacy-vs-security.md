# Privacy vs. Security

**Security** protects data from unauthorized access and harm; **privacy** governs the **appropriate collection and use** of **personal data**.

You can be secure yet still misuse data. Privacy requires security to work, with core principles like:

- **Data minimization**: Collect only what you need.
- **Purpose limitation**: Use data only for the stated purpose.
- **Individual rights**: Access, correction, and deletion of one's data.
- **Breach notification**: Inform regulators and affected individuals in a timely manner (e.g., GDPR's 72-hour rule).

## Remember
- Security and privacy are distinct obligations that work together.
- Privacy focuses on the appropriate use of personal data.
- Data minimization and purpose limitation are central privacy concepts.
