# The Risk Management Cycle

Since you cannot eliminate all risk, you **manage** it deliberately through a continuous loop:

1.  **Identify** assets and the risks to them.
2.  **Assess** each risk by **likelihood × impact** and rank them.
3.  **Treat** each risk by choosing a response (see: Risk Treatment Options).
4.  **Monitor** and review continuously.

The process ensures that security efforts are focused on the highest-priority risks to the organization.

## Remember
- Risk management is a cycle, not a one-time activity.
- Assessment is based on likelihood and impact.
- Continuous monitoring is essential for effective risk management.
