# Compliance is a Floor

Passing a compliance audit means you met a **baseline** on a given day — it is **not proof of security**.

Attackers do not care whether you're compliant. Aim for **genuine risk reduction**; treat compliance as a minimum to clear, and beware the **checkbox trap** of doing the paperwork while real risks go unaddressed.

## Remember
- Compliance is a point-in-time floor, not the goal.
- Real security is a continuous process of risk management.
- Do not let compliance checklists distract from real, unaddressed risks.
