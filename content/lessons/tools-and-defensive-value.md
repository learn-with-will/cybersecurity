Professionals use tools — like **Nmap** (discovery), **Burp Suite** (web proxy), and **Metasploit** (exploitation) — exclusively against authorized targets.

These tools are not just for attackers. Defenders use the *same* tools to perform their own audits and understand their attack surface. 

- **Pen testing (depth)** complements **vulnerability scanning (breadth)**.
- Automated scans find many issues quickly.
- Skilled humans chain those issues together to show real-world impact that scanners miss.
- Both activities inform better hardening and stronger defenses.
