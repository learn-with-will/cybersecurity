# Containment, Eradication, and Recovery

Once an incident is identified, the technical response begins:

-   **Containment**: Stop the bleeding.
    -   *Short-term*: Isolate affected hosts from the network.
    -   *Long-term*: Temporary fixes to keep business running.
    -   *Key*: Do not tip off an active attacker before you are ready to remove them.
-   **Eradication**: Remove the threat.
    -   Delete malware, disable compromised accounts, and close exploited vulnerabilities.
    -   *Best practice*: Rebuild from known-good images rather than "cleaning" a system.
-   **Recovery**: Restore to normal.
    -   Restore systems from **trusted backups**.
    -   Monitor closely for re-entry.
