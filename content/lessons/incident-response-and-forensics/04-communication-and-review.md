# Communication and Post-Incident Review

Incidents involve more than just technology; they involve stakeholders.

### Roles & Communication
-   **Incident Commander**: Person coordinating the response.
-   **Stakeholders**: Legal, PR, management, and potentially customers/regulators.
-   *Tip*: Decide on communication channels in advance (assume internal systems are compromised).

### Post-Incident Review
The "lessons learned" review is where an incident pays off.
-   **Goal**: Identify concrete improvements (e.g., missing control, slow step).
-   **Blameless**: Conduct review without blame to ensure people share the truth.
-   **Feedback Loop**: Updates the Preparation phase, closing the loop.
