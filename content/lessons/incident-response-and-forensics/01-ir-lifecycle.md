# The Incident-Response Lifecycle

NIST describes incident response (IR) as a repeating lifecycle. A common phrasing:

1.  **Preparation**: Plans, tools, training, contacts — done **before** anything.
2.  **Detection & Analysis**: Recognize and scope the incident.
3.  **Containment, Eradication & Recovery**: Stop the spread, remove the threat, restore safely.
4.  **Post-Incident Activity**: "Lessons learned" to improve.

The most underrated phase is **Preparation**: the plan, roles, and communication tree must be rehearsed *before* a real event.
