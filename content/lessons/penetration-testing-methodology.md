Most methodologies (e.g., **PTES**, the **OWASP WSTG**) follow the same arc:

1.  **Reconnaissance**: Gather public info (in scope only).
2.  **Scanning/Enumeration**: Map hosts, ports, services.
3.  **Gaining Access**: Safely demonstrate exploitation of a real weakness.
4.  **Post-exploitation**: Assess impact (what data/systems are reachable?).
5.  **Reporting**: Document findings, evidence, risk, and fixes.
6.  **Remediation retest**: Confirm the fixes actually work.

Recon and scanning are the same techniques defenders use to understand their own attack surface. The professional avoids causing outages or touching data they don't need to.
