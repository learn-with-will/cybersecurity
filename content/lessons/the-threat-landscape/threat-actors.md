> **In one line:** Understanding who attacks and why helps in prioritizing defenses against their likely strategies.

## The idea

A **threat actor** is the entity causing a threat. Knowing who they are and what they want is crucial:

- **Script Kiddies**: Low skill, use existing tools to find easy targets.
- **Cybercriminals**: Motivated by money, focus on high-impact targets like banks.
- **Nation-States**: High resources, focus on political or industrial espionage.
- **Insider Threats**: Employees or contractors with privileged access.

## See it

| Actor | Motivation | Resource Level |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Script Kiddie | Fame/Fun | Low |
| Criminal | Profit | Moderate/High |
| Nation-State | Espionage/Power | Extreme |

## Remember
- Threat actors have varied motivations, from profit to espionage.
- Knowing your likely adversary helps tailor your defense strategy.
- Insider threats remain a significant, often overlooked, challenge.
