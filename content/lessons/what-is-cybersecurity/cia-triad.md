> **In one line:** The CIA triad—confidentiality, integrity, and availability—is the core framework for all security goals.

## The idea

The three classic goals of security are remembered as the CIA triad. Every security control you learn will serve one or more of these goals:

- **Confidentiality**: Information is seen only by authorized users. Broken by leaks. Protected by encryption and access control.
- **Integrity**: Information is accurate and untampered with. Broken by unauthorized changes. Protected by hashes and signatures.
- **Availability**: Information and services are usable when needed. Broken by outages or denial of service. Protected by backups and redundancy.

## Remember
- Confidentiality ensures data is seen only by authorized parties.
- Integrity guarantees that information is accurate and has not been tampered with.
- Availability ensures services are usable when needed.
