> **In one line:** Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting systems and information from digital attacks, making them harder and more expensive to breach.

## The idea

Cybersecurity protects systems, networks, programs, and data from digital attack, damage, or unauthorized access. The goal is not to make a system impossible to attack — which is unachievable — but to make attacks hard, expensive, and detectable enough that your assets stay protected against the threats you face.

Security is a trade-off. Perfect security would mean a system that is turned off, encased in concrete, and unusable. Real security balances protection against cost, usability, and performance.

## See it

| Objective | Protection Goal |
| :--- | :--- |
| Confidentiality | Info only seen by authorized users |
| Integrity | Info accurate and untampered |
| Availability | Services usable when needed |

## Remember
- Cybersecurity is the protection of systems and data from digital harm.
- The goal is to manage risk, not to achieve impossible perfection.
- Security is a balancing act between protection, cost, and usability.
