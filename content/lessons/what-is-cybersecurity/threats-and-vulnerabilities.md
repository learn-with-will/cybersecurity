> **In one line:** Precise definitions of assets, threats, vulnerabilities, and risk allow for clear reasoning about security needs.

## The idea

Security has a precise vocabulary. Confusing these leads to muddled thinking:

- **Asset**: Something of value worth protecting (data, servers).
- **Vulnerability**: A weakness that could be exploited (unpatched bug, weak password).
- **Threat**: A potential cause of harm (criminal, insider, natural disaster).
- **Exploit**: The technique or code that takes advantage of a vulnerability.
- **Risk**: The likelihood of a threat hitting a vulnerability, multiplied by the impact of that event: `risk ≈ likelihood × impact`.

## See it

| Term | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| Asset | Value to protect |

## Remember
- An asset is something valuable that needs protection.
- A vulnerability is the weakness, while a threat is the potential cause of harm.
- Risk is informally defined as likelihood multiplied by impact.
