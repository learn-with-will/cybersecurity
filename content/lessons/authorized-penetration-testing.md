---

Before any technical work, ethical hacking requires **explicit, written permission** from the system owner. This isn't a formality — it's the line between a security service and a computer-crime offense.

A proper engagement establishes:

- **Scope** — exactly which systems, addresses, and applications are in and out of bounds.
- **Rules of Engagement (RoE)** — allowed techniques, testing windows, data-handling rules, and what to do if something breaks.
- **Authorization document** ("get-out-of-jail-free letter") — signed proof the testing is sanctioned.
- **Contacts and safety** — who to call, and limits to avoid outages or harm.

Sticking to scope and RoE is non-negotiable; stepping outside them is both unethical and illegal. Curiosity is not authorization.
